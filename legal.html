4 · Simulator & modelled-output disclaimer

The Site's "digital twin" simulators (energy balance, bill impact, grid flexibility, and any similar tools) are policy illustrations. They are deliberately simplified models with user-adjustable, openly disclosed assumptions. In particular:

  • Outputs are illustrative orders of magnitude, not engineering studies, load-flow analyses, tariff determinations, rate cases, or financial projections.
  • Coefficients derive from the self-reported deployment figures described above; infrastructure-cost inputs are presented as wide, jurisdiction-dependent ranges.
  • Stylised curves (for example, daily demand and solar profiles) are archetypes, not measurements of any actual grid.
  • Generated "term sheets" are drafting aids for discussion. They are not contracts, offers, regulatory filings, or legal instruments, and create no rights or obligations for anyone.
  • Results depend entirely on the inputs you choose. Do not present outputs as findings about any actual facility, utility, or jurisdiction without independent professional analysis.

5 · Privacy policy

Plain-language summary

We collect nothing. No accounts, no forms, no cookies set by us, no analytics, no ads, no fingerprinting. The only storage used is a single session flag in your own browser so the intro animation doesn't replay — it never leaves your device.

5.1 Data we collect

None. The Site is a static website. We do not collect, store, process, sell or share personal data. There are no user accounts, no contact forms, no newsletters, no embedded trackers, and no advertising or analytics scripts.

5.2 Local storage

The Site sets one sessionStorage value in your browser (a flag noting the intro animation has played, plus any simulator inputs you adjust, which live only in the page while it is open). This information is stored on your device only, is never transmitted to us or anyone else, and is cleared by your browser automatically. No consent banner is required for strictly functional local storage of this kind, and we show none.

The Site is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web host, GitHub may log technical request data (such as IP addresses) for security and operational purposes; that processing is governed by GitHub's own privacy documentation, not by us, and we have no access to identifying visitor logs. See GitHub's General Privacy Statement and its Pages data-collection notes.

5.4 External links

Links to third-party sites (for example, iea.org or github.com) lead to services with their own privacy practices. We are not responsible for them.

5.5 Children

The Site is general-audience informational content and collects no data from anyone, including children.

5.6 Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or erase. If you believe otherwise, contact us (section 7) and we will respond promptly. You may also clear your browser's local storage at any time.
